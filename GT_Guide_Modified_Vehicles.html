Save Wrenches for Vehicle Day

Golden Wrenches are the primary resource for vehicle modification. Save ALL wrenches throughout the week and use them ONLY on Alliance Duel Day 1 (Vehicle Day) for maximum event credit. Using wrenches any other day wastes Duel points.

Modifier Progression
1

Expert Modifier (Level 100-130)

First major milestone. Unlocks at 4% and scales to 100%. Provides meaningful combat bonuses and signals mid-game progression.

~2,150 - 7,700 Cumulative Wrenches
2

Master Modifier (Level 135-160)

Significant stat bonuses. The Destroyer unlock at level 145 is a major power spike. This is where vehicle modification starts meaningfully impacting PvP.

~8,500 - 16,000 Cumulative Wrenches
3

Grandmaster Modifier (Level 165-200)

Late-game territory. Destroyer-EX unlock at 195. Wrench costs per sub-level jump to 240-300+. This is whale territory for most players.

~18,500 - 38,000+ Cumulative Wrenches
4

Modding Overlord (Level 200+)

Endgame. Costs jump to 360-420 wrenches per sub-level with 10 sub-levels each. Only the most dedicated or heavy-spending players reach this tier.

Pro Tips
📅 Day 1 Only: Save ALL golden wrenches, blueprints, and chip chests throughout the week. Open and use everything exclusively on Alliance Duel Day 1 (Vehicle Day).
📊 Track Progress: Use the wrench cost table to estimate how long until your next vehicle unlock. Set realistic expectations — Destroyer requires ~10,500 total wrenches.
🎯 Milestone Focus: The biggest power spikes come at vehicle unlock levels (Cheetah, Hercules, Destroyer). Plan your wrench spending around hitting these milestones.
💎 Blueprint Value: Modification Blueprints are rarer than wrenches. Save them for Vehicle Day as well — they provide significant upgrade progress and count for Duel points.
