Bounty Hunting
In Other States

Learn how to travel to foreign states and find the best bounties to maximize your rewards.

Bounty Types
💎
Blue

Common bounties. Lower rewards but plentiful — good for volume farming.

🔮
Purple

Uncommon bounties. Better rewards with a good balance of availability.

Best 🟠
Orange

Rare bounties. Highest rewards. Always prioritize these when available.

6

Level 6 = The Best

Bounties are numbered by quality. Level 6 is the highest tier available. Always aim for Level 6 Orange bounties for maximum reward. Don't waste your energy on low-level targets if better ones are available.

Step-by-Step Guide
1

Open the Search

Tap the magnifying glass icon in the bottom left corner of your screen to open the state search.

🔍
Magnifying Glass — Bottom LeftTap this to open the state navigation panel.
2

Enter Your Destination State

In the first box, you'll see your current state number (e.g. 174). Clear it and type the number of the state you want to travel to.

Example: Change 174 → 209
3

Confirm the Travel

Tap the magnifying glass again to confirm. You will now be viewing the target state's map.

4

Zoom Out to Find Large Cities

Zoom out on the map to see all the cities in the state. You're looking for the largest cities — that's where the biggest alliances live, and where the best bounties spawn.

🏙️
Big City = Big BountiesLarge alliances attract higher-level bounties. Skip small or empty cities.
5

Zoom In and Browse

Once you've found a large city, zoom in to see the available bounties. Look for the color and level that fits your goal.

6

Select Your Target

Tap the bounty you want to engage. Prioritize Orange Level 6 whenever possible for the best return on your resources.

Pro Tips
🗺️ Not every state will have great bounties. If a state looks empty or low quality, move on and try another one.
⚡ Bounties refresh every few hours. If you don't find anything good the first time, check back later — new bounties will appear.
⚔️ SvS Strategy: During State vs State week, hunt bounties in our opponent's state. Steal their resources and weaken them at the same time — two birds, one stone.
📢 Share the Wealth: If you find a high quality bounty — typically 12+ orange books — post it in the Trucks/Bounties alliance chat so other members can come loot it too. We win together.
