Building Your Formation
1

Pick One Faction

Choose Blood Rose or Wings of Dawn for F2P. These factions have the most accessible heroes. Stick to one — don't mix factions.

F2P Recommendation: Blood Rose or Wings of Dawn
2

Place Defense Heroes in Front Row

Heroes tagged "strong defense" go in the front. They absorb damage and protect your back row. Never put an attack-type hero in front — it's the #1 formation mistake.

3

Place DPS Heroes in Back Row

Attack and DPS heroes go in the back 3 slots. They deal the majority of your damage while being protected by your front line.

4

Match Troop Type to Faction

Blood Rose = train Assaulters. Wings of Dawn = train Shooters. Guard of Order = train Riders. Their 3rd and 4th skills specifically boost these troop types.

5

Get All 5 to 4-Star

4★ unlocks the 4th skill for each hero — this is the biggest power spike. Get all 5 formation heroes to 4★ before pushing anyone to 5★.

Pro Tips
🦅 Counter Strategy: Wings of Dawn beats Blood Rose. Since most F2P players run Blood Rose, a full WoD team naturally counters the majority of opponents you'll face.
🚫 Common Mistake: Never put attack-type heroes (Oliveira, Scarlett) in the front row. They'll die fast and your formation collapses. Defense heroes ONLY in front.
💰 Resource Focus: All EXP, skill books, power cores, and equipment go to your main 5 ONLY. Do not spread resources across 10+ heroes — concentrate everything.
⚖️ Guard of Order Warning: GoO heroes are powerful but hard to collect fragments for as F2P. Only build GoO if you're a spender or very patient.
