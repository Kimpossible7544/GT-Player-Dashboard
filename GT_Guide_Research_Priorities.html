Research Priorities

The optimal laboratory research order to maximize your growth without wasting badges or time.

Second Lab — Only If You Can Afford It

Two research queues means double the speed — but the second lab costs real money or significant in-game currency. Only invest in a second lab if you can comfortably afford it. If you do get it: run badge-free trees in Lab 1 continuously, and badge-heavy trees in Lab 2 timed to Alliance Duel events.

Research Order — Badge-Free First
1
Shelter Building
No Badges
Max this first. No badges required. Provides construction speed and resource protection. This is where every player should start — fast, free, and immediately impactful.
Unlock: Available Immediately
2
HQ Management → Rapid Growth
No Badges
Max HQ Management, which unlocks Rapid Growth. Max that too. Still no badges needed. These give construction speed, resource production, and troop training buffs that compound over time.
Unlock: After Shelter Building
3
Elite Troops + Allied Troops
No Badges
Run these in parallel if you have two labs. They boost troop stats and training capacity. Still badge-free, so there's no reason not to push them early.
Unlock: After Rapid Growth
Badge Trees — Time to Events
4
Alliance Recognition — Complete Early!
Badges Required
This is your most important badge investment — complete it as early as possible. Alliance Recognition unlocks chests 4-9 in Alliance Duel, doubles badge rewards, and dramatically increases the value of every future Alliance Duel week. Every week you delay this research costs you thousands of badges in missed rewards. Complete high-cost nodes during Day 3 (Research Day) of Alliance Duel for double credit.
Key Unlock: Premium Rewards (1,200 badges) → Super Rewards (4,100 badges)
5
Hero Training → Military Strategies → Peace Shield
Badges Required
After Alliance Recognition is solid: push Hero Training to Cockpit unlock, then Military Strategies for troop HP buffs, then Peace Shield for Urgent Rescue (-20% losses). These three form your combat research foundation.
Key Unlock: Urgent Rescue = -20% Troop Losses
Two-Lab Strategy
🔬 Lab 1 (Badge-Free): Cycle Shelter Building → HQ Management → Rapid Growth → Elite Troops → Allied Troops continuously. Never let this lab sit idle.
🏅 Lab 2 (Badge Trees): Reserved for Alliance Recognition, Hero Training, Military Strategies, and later UST. Time completions to Alliance Duel Day 3 for maximum event credit.
📅 Event Timing: Start badge research mid-week so it completes during the Day 3 Research window. Finishing research during the ranking period earns both Duel points AND badge rewards simultaneously.
⚡ Speed-Up Rule: Save research speed-ups exclusively for Day 3 of Alliance Duel. Using them any other day wastes potential Duel points.
Common Mistakes
❌ Rushing toward T10/UST early. The badge cost is 1.4 million+ — you need Alliance Recognition maxed first to earn badges fast enough.
❌ Spending badges outside event windows. Every badge-cost research completion should happen during Alliance Duel Research Day.
❌ Letting labs sit idle. Even a 5-minute research node is better than zero. Queue something always.
