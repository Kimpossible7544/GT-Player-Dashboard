Golden Hours Explained
⭐

What Are Golden Hours?

Each day has two 4-hour windows called Golden Hours. During these windows, you earn points for both Full Preparedness AND Duel simultaneously. This means every item you use, every building you complete, and every troop you train counts double toward your score. Always try to time your item use within these windows.

Weekly Schedule
🔧 MON

Wrenches & Blueprints ⭐ Golden Hours

Use all Wrenches and Blueprints on Monday. These items are used for Vehicle Modification upgrades. Save them through the weekend and deploy them during Monday's Golden Hours for maximum points.

🏗️ TUE

Building & Construction ⭐ Golden Hours

Tuesday is for all Building activity. Queue your construction projects and use Building Speed-Ups to complete them during Tuesday's Golden Hours.

🔬 WED

Research ⭐ Golden Hours

Wednesday is Research day. Start and complete Research during Wednesday's Golden Hours.

🦸 THU

Hero Items ⭐ Golden Hours

Thursday is for Hero Items. Use any items that level up, upgrade, or enhance your Hero on this day. Timing these during Thursday's Golden Hours earns you the most points.

⚔️ FRI

Troop Training ⭐ Golden Hours

Friday is Troop Training day. Train all troops and use Training Speed-Ups during Friday's Golden Hours.

🛡️ SAT — SUN

SvS & Enemy Buster — Hold Your Items

Saturday and Sunday are for State vs State and Enemy Buster events. Do not use construction, research, training, or other speed-ups during the weekend. Save everything for the following Monday. Focus on SvS strategy and Enemy Buster targets.

Full Preparedness

Complete daily at Apocalypse Time (08:00 or 20:00). Earn merit chests every day. When its theme aligns with Alliance Duel theme = Golden Hour. Never miss this.

Your main source of badges, hero fragments, and alliance rewards. Save resources all week, deploy only on matching days. Coordinate with alliance for maximum output.

Speed-Up Rules
⏱️
Never use speed-ups randomly. Always align with the matching Alliance Duel day. Construction speed-ups → Day 2. Research speed-ups → Day 3. Training speed-ups → Day 5.
⚡
Golden Hour doubles everything. When Full Prep theme aligns with Duel theme (typically at 08:00 and 20:00 Apocalypse Time), deploy your biggest speed-ups here for 2x rewards.
📊
150% construction buff is the sweet spot. Diminishing returns kick in after this. Don't chase higher buffs — they're inefficient.
🏗️
Save for big upgrades. HQ, Laboratory, and Troop Camps benefit most from speed-ups. Don't waste them on low-level buildings.
Golden Rules
⭐ Always use items during Golden Hours. Each day has two 4-hour Golden Hour windows. Using items outside these windows wastes potential points.
📅 Stick to the weekly schedule. Using items on the right day ensures you earn Full Preparedness points. Discipline is the difference between top scores and wasted effort.
🚫 Never use speed-ups on weekends. Saturday and Sunday are SvS and Enemy Buster days. Save all items for Monday's restart of the weekly cycle.
💡 Plan ahead. If you know you won't be online during Golden Hours, try to queue tasks just before so they complete during that window, or ask an alliance leader for guidance.
