Focus on Orange Gear First

Don't invest heavily in green or blue gear upgrades. Save your materials and fragments for purple and orange gear. Upgrading low-tier gear wastes resources you'll need later. Use whatever you find early on, but don't enhance anything below purple.

Upgrade Priority Order
1

Equip Full Orange Set on Main Heroes

Before enhancing anything, make sure all 5 heroes in your main formation have a complete set of orange gear. An unenhanced orange piece outperforms an enhanced blue piece. Collect fragments from campaign, events, and the gear workshop.

Full orange set > partially enhanced blue set
2

Gun First

Your DPS heroes benefit most from gun upgrades. These boost attack damage which directly increases your kill speed. Prioritize this slot on your strongest damage dealer.

3

Then Helmet

Helmet upgrades provide critical HP and defense stats that keep your heroes alive longer. Enhance this next to improve survivability across your formation.

4

Then Vest

The vest adds defense and HP to further protect your heroes. A solid vest upgrade helps your front-row tanks survive extended fights.

5

Boots Last

March speed and mixed stats from boots are useful but not urgent. Fill this slot out after your core combat gear is enhanced.

6

Refine to Gold When Ready

Once you have enhanced orange gear, you can refine pieces to gold quality using refinement materials. This is a late-game investment — only refine gear you plan to use permanently. Never refine before you've committed to a set.

Only refine gear you'll keep long-term
Gear Sets to Target
🗡️
Assaulter Set
Set Bonus: +Assaulter ATK, +Assaulter HP
Best for assaulter-heavy formations. Boosts your front-line troops' attack and durability. Pair with assaulter heroes like Laura or Katrina.
PvP
🎯
Shooter Set
Set Bonus: +Shooter ATK, +Shooter Crit
Ideal for shooter-focused formations. Maximizes ranged damage output. Great with heroes like Fiona or Isabella in the back row.
PvP
🏍️
Rider Set
Set Bonus: +Rider ATK, +March Speed
For rider-based formations. Adds march speed which is useful for rallies and quick strikes. Niche but powerful in the right comp.
PvP
🛡️
Defense Set
Set Bonus: +All Troop DEF, +HP
Great for base defense and garrison heroes. Makes your base harder to raid. Use on your wall defense formation leader.
Defense
⚖️
Balanced Set
Set Bonus: +All Troop ATK, +All Troop DEF
Jack-of-all-trades set that works with any troop type. Good starter set if you haven't committed to a troop specialization yet.
