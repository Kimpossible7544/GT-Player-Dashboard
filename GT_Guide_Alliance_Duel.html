Win Rewards vs. Loss Rewards

Winning gives 1,500 badges + 6 orange hero fragments + 1,000 diamonds. Losing still nets 1,000 badges. Always participate — even a loss is profitable. Over a 4-week cycle, top-tier winners earn up to 20,000 badges.

6-Day Schedule
1
Vehicle Day
Save: Golden wrenches, blueprints, chip chests
Open all saved vehicle items. Use wrenches for modifications. Clear saved radar tasks. Everything vehicle-related goes today.
2
Building Day
Save: Construction speed-ups, orange refugees
Queue your biggest building upgrades. Use construction speed-ups to complete them. One of the harder days to score big — stockpile aggressively.
3
Research Day
Save: Research speed-ups, badges
Complete high-cost badge research nodes today. Time research to finish during this window. Prioritize orange trade trucks. This is your best badge ROI day.
4
Heroes Day
Save: Hero fragments, cores, skill books
Level up heroes, use skill books, apply power cores. Save Club recruitment tickets for this day. Everything hero-related goes here.
5
Training Day
Save: Training speed-ups
Mass troop production. Queue as many troop training batches as possible. Use all training speed-ups. Maximize troop count output.
6
Combat Day (Enemy Buster)
Save: Bounty missions, attack opportunities
Kill enemies, complete bounty missions, attack rival bases. Stack all combat and PvP actions for this day. Coordinate with alliance for group attacks.
Alliance Recognition Research

Priority Research Nodes

Event Expert (20 levels) 18,210 badges +100% event points
Premium Rewards (1 level) 1,200 badges Unlocks chests 4-6
Super Rewards (1 level) 4,100 badges Unlocks chests 7-9
Small Nodes (4 × 10 levels) 8,300 each March speed, load, training
Full Max (Event King) ~143,500 total Maximum rewards
Competitive Strategy
1

Rush Event Expert First

+100% event points is the single biggest multiplier. Every badge spent here pays for itself in doubled future Duel scores. This is your first Alliance Recognition priority.

+100% Event Points = Everything Doubled
2

Unlock All Chest Tiers

Premium Rewards (1,200 badges) → chests 4-6. Super Rewards (4,100 badges) → chests 7-9. Without these, even hitting 6M points leaves rewards on the table.

3

Hit Golden Hour Windows

When Alliance Duel theme aligns with Full Preparedness (typically at Apocalypse Time 08:00 or 20:00), deploy your biggest items. Same resources score in BOTH events. Split deployments across both windows if Golden Hour occurs twice.

4

Coordinate with Alliance

Post the weekly schedule before it starts. Announce Golden Hour times daily. Members who know the schedule save correctly. One pre-week message prevents most waste.

Pro Tips
🏅 Badge Math: Base earning is ~830 badges/day × 6 days = ~5,000/week. With Alliance Recognition doubling rewards: ~10,000/week. Plus win bonuses of 1,500. Max this research ASAP.
📅 Top 30 in Your Alliance: Finishing in the top 30 within your alliance on any given day gives extra daily rewards. Push hard on days where you have the most saved resources to guarantee a top-30 spot in your alliance's leaderboard.
💡 Zero Outside Theme: Items used outside their matching theme day score ZERO Duel points. This is the #1 mistake new players make. Save EVERYTHING for the right day.
🤝 Alliance Wins Matter: Over a 4-week cycle, consistent wins compound into massive badge and fragment advantages. Every member participating matters — even small contributions add up.
