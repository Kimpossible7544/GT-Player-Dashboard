Hero Investment Guide

Know which heroes to upgrade, which to skip, and how to maximize your resources for the strongest formations.

Hero Ranks
Best 👑
S+ Rank

Strongest heroes. Invest everything here. Hard to obtain but game-changing.

⭐
A+ Rank

Solid heroes. Great for F2P players. Worth full investment if no S+ Rank available.

💀
B

Tutorial heroes only. Do NOT invest EXP, skill books, or cores into these.

🚫

Never Invest in B Heroes

Angelina and William are B tutorial heroes. They seem decent early on, but they fall off hard. Every resource you put into them is wasted. Save your EXP, skill books, and power cores for A+ Rank and S+ Rank heroes only.

Upgrade Priority Order
1

Get All Formation Heroes to 4-Star

4-Star unlocks each hero's 4th skill — this is a massive power spike. Get all 5 heroes in your main formation to 4★ before pushing any single hero to 5★.

4★ = 4th Skill Unlock = Big Power Spike
2

Max Your Main Formation First

Pick 5 heroes from the same faction and pour all resources into them. EXP, skill books, power cores, and equipment — all go to your main 5 only.

3

Focus EXP on DPS Heroes First

Your back-row damage dealers benefit most from early EXP investment. Level them first, then bring tanks/support up to match.

4

Save Club Tickets for Events

Don't spend recruitment tickets one by one. Save them and use during Hero Day (Day 4) of Alliance Duel for double value.

5

Never Spread Resources Thin

A single maxed team of 5 will always beat 15 half-leveled heroes. Resist the urge to upgrade everyone. Concentrate resources.

Pro Tips
🎯 F2P Priority: Focus on Blood Rose (Katrina, Sophia) or Wings of Dawn (Laura, Fiona). These factions have the most accessible S+/A+ Rank heroes for free players.
⚠️ Avoid Guard of Order: Unless you're a heavy spender, GoO heroes are hard to collect fragments for. Their faction bonus is excellent but unrealistic for F2P.
💡 Stage 5 Sophia: The $1 Sophia offer at Stage 5 is the single best purchase in the game. If you spend any money at all, buy this.
Best Purchases for Low-Spending Players

💰 Spending Smart — Maximum Value for Minimum Cost

If you're going to spend a little, these are the purchases that give you the biggest advantage. Listed in order of priority:

1

Stage 5 Sophia Offer — $1

The single best purchase in the game. Unlocks Sophia early, who is an S+ Rank hero. This alone can carry your entire early game.

Best value in the entire game — do not skip this
2

Monthly Supply Card — ~$5

Gives daily diamonds and speed-ups for 30 days. Over a month, this adds up to far more value than any single gem pack. Consistent daily income accelerates everything.

3

Growth Fund — ~$10

One-time purchase that pays out diamonds as you reach HQ milestones. The total payout far exceeds the cost. Buy early so you collect rewards as you progress.

4

Second Builder Queue — Diamonds

Not a direct purchase, but use your earned diamonds to unlock the second builder. Having two buildings upgrade simultaneously doubles your progression speed.

5

Event-Only Bundles

Wait for special event packs that include hero fragments, speed-ups, and resources. These rotate and offer 3-5x the value of standard store packs. Never buy standard diamond packs.

Standard store packs are a trap — always wait for event bundles
🚫 Avoid: Random gem packs, gacha hero pulls (outside events), VIP point bundles, and any "limited time" pack that doesn't include hero fragments or speed-ups. These drain money with minimal return.
