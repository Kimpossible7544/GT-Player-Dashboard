Base Defense & Raiding

How to protect your resources from attackers, and how to efficiently raid enemy bases for maximum loot.

⚠️

High-Risk Windows

Enemy Buster events, State Ruler, and cross-server events dramatically increase attack frequency. Shield up before these windows. Keep 2,000 diamonds as emergency shield money at all times.

Defense Fundamentals
🏦
Warehouse Protection
Resources below Warehouse capacity CANNOT be raided. Check your threshold — only shield when stockpile exceeds it.
🏗️
Commit Resources
Queue a long building or training batch before going offline. Resources actively being consumed cannot be raided.
🦸
Wall Formation
Wall defense uses your active hero formation. Switch to your highest ATK/HP combat formation before logging off.
🔙
Recall Troops
Troops on marches or gathering can't defend your base. Recall all troops before going offline near risky events.
Offline Defense Checklist
1

Check Warehouse Capacity

If your resources are below the Warehouse limit, you're already safe from resource theft. No shield needed for resources.

Below Warehouse = Cannot Be Stolen
2

Spend Excess Resources

Queue building upgrades, troop training, or research to consume surplus. Committed resources are untouchable. This is better than shielding defensively.

3

Recall All Marches

Bring all troops home. Troops gathering or exploring cannot defend your wall. You want maximum garrison strength before logging off.

4

Set Combat Formation

Switch to your strongest combat formation — not resource or research heroes. Your wall pulls from whatever formation is currently active.

5

Shield If Needed

During high-risk events (Enemy Buster, SvS, weekend raids), activate a shield. Buy from Alliance Shop first — save diamonds for emergencies.

Raiding Tips
1

Always Scout First

Never attack blind. Scout to check power level, troop count, and resource stores. If they have more power than you, skip them.

🔍
Scout Before Every AttackOnly attack bases visibly over-stored on resources. Hitting a well-managed base yields nothing.
2

Target Over-Stored Bases

Look for bases with visible resource piles (farms, wood stacks) in their city view. These players have resources above their Warehouse protection limit.

3

Only Attack Weaker Targets

Attacking a stronger base wastes your troops in the hospital. Only raid bases with less power than you. Efficiency over aggression.

4

Time Raids to Enemy Buster

During Enemy Buster events (typically Saturdays), you earn event points for attacking. Stack your raids during this window for double value.

Enemy Buster = Raid Points + Loot
Pro Tips
💎 Diamond Rule: Diamonds cannot be raided — they're always safe. The 2,000 diamond reserve is for emergency shields, not resource protection.
🤝 Alliance Reinforcements: If you're being rallied, request alliance reinforcements immediately. Multiple alliance members can garrison your base to boost your defense.
📍 Relocation: If you're being targeted repeatedly, use a random relocation to move your base. Don't sit still and lose troops every day.
⚔️ SvS Raiding: During State vs State, prioritize raiding the enemy state. You earn SvS points AND steal their resources — double value for every march.
