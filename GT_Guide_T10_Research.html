This is a Late-Game Goal

Do NOT rush T10 early. The full Unit Special Training branch costs 1,488,100 badges. You need Alliance Recognition maxed first to earn badges fast enough. Without it, you'll stall for months. Complete your foundation research first (see Research Priorities guide).

Prerequisites
1

Laboratory Level 30

UST branch doesn't appear until your Lab is level 30. This alone requires significant HQ progression and resources to reach.

Lab 30 = Minimum Requirement
2

Alliance Recognition Maxed

This doubles your badge income from Alliance Duel. Without it, earning 1.49M badges takes twice as long. Always max this BEFORE starting UST.

3

Massive Badge Stockpile

You need hundreds of thousands of badges before starting. Begin stockpiling once Alliance Recognition is complete. Target: 100k+ badges before you begin the first UST node.

UST Research Path
Early Nodes (6-7 nodes) ~90-150K Badges
Starts at 790-1,300 badges per level. These are affordable and provide initial troop stat buffs. Good early progress that builds momentum.
Mid Tree Branches ~200-300K Badges
Melee Combat II, Weapon Upgrading II, Stronger Armor II, Shield Upgrade II, Camo Coating II — each costs 116,500 badges for 15 levels. This is where the real badge wall begins.
Recharge Shield + Late Nodes ~219K Badges
Recharge Shield alone is 219,000 badges for 20 levels. This provides late PvP scaling but is extremely expensive. Only pursue if T10 is your definite goal.
Final: Unit Special Training 54,900 Badges
The final unlock node. Only 54,900 badges, but you must complete everything before it. Plus 8-14 days of speed-ups and millions of resources to actually finish.
Badge Earning Strategy
🏅 Weekly Badge Income: With maxed Alliance Recognition: ~8,300-10,000 badges/week from Alliance Duel alone. Without it: roughly half that. Max it first.
📅 Timeline Estimate: At 10,000 badges/week, reaching 1.49M takes approximately 149 weeks (~2.8 years). This is a marathon, not a sprint. Plan accordingly.
💎 Badge Sources: Alliance Duel (primary), Alliance Win bonuses (1,500/week if winning), daily victory rewards, and special events. Maximize all sources.
⚡ Speed-Up Planning: Each deep UST node takes 1-8 days of raw research time. Stockpile research speed-ups and deploy them on Day 3 of Alliance Duel for double credit.
Common Mistakes
❌ Starting UST before maxing Alliance Recognition. You'll earn badges at half speed and the path takes twice as long.
❌ Neglecting foundation research (Hero Training, Military Strategies, Peace Shield) to "rush T10." Your combat effectiveness suffers massively.
❌ Spending badges outside event windows. Every badge-cost completion should happen during Alliance Duel Day 3 for maximum credit.
